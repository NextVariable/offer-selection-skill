# PhD and academic path

First identify the discipline's training/admission model.

- PI-driven: supervisor/lab fit, funding, group quality, research access, and placement may dominate.
- Programme-driven: research-program quality, advanced methods, dissertation, faculty environment/fit, progression, and placement may dominate.

General order: research goal → discipline training model → programme-level research pathway → model-appropriate supervisor/faculty/lab fit → accessible research opportunity → research-capital formation → discipline strength → evidenced research-ecosystem/geography prior → overall brand → cost/risk → recommendation.

Research whether the route is taught MSc, research master, MRes/MPhil, or integrated PhD; inspect methods training, substantial dissertation, cohort intensity, group participation, realistic RA access, supervision capacity, outputs/references, funding, progression, historical PhD placement, and exit options. Faculty/lab existence does not guarantee access.

For committed PhD intent, materially stronger research transformation can outweigh overall university rank. For uncertain intent, separately evaluate academic preparation and employment fallback; longer integrated routes can be academically superior but costlier and less flexible.

For STEM, engineering, CS, natural science, quantitative, and other technical disciplines, an evidenced U.S. research-ecosystem prior may apply (research-university density, labs/groups, faculty/PI choice, thesis routes, RA/projects, PhD continuity, industry R&D connections). It is conditional: stronger only when the compared programmes are materially similar on pathway quality, fit, accessible opportunity, cost, eligibility, and personal constraints; a professional, course-only, cash-cow, or no-thesis/no-lab/no-RA U.S. master's receives no automatic PhD advantage; a stronger ETH/EPFL/Cambridge/Imperial/Oxford, European research master, MRes, MPhil, or other programme-level pathway dominates it; non-STEM is case by case. Full definition and boundaries: `domain/priors-and-calibration.md`. Use is governed by core ("Priors as conditional evidence").

Ask: “What research candidate does this offer create, and does it materially improve research capital and access to the target PhD/academic path?” Do not promise admission probabilities without defensible data.
